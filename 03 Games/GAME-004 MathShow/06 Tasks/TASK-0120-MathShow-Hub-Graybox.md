All seven are inside 8-12 s from the plaza centre. Spawn to Daily is 13.0 s, the only outlier, and that route is not the target; a shorter ramp or a pad closer to the plaza would fix it if it matters.

Reachability: pathfinding found a route to all 34 GlyphSpawn markers (21 common, 13 rare), 0 unreachable. `character_navigation` also walked the character to Pad_Daily, Roof2, Tier5 (top tier, 12 studs), Terrace3 and Lake2, and Roof2, Tier5 and Terrace3 were confirmed within 2.4 studs of the target.

Performance (Play, 1047x669 viewport, current place lighting): 59.6 fps average (16.8 ms), worst frame 46.8 ms, 340 visible hub parts (332 block, 7 cylinder, 1 ball), 0 lights, about 6k triangles (estimate from shape counts). Total hub BaseParts: 423.
