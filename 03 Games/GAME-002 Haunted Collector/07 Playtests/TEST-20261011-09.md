- `HauntedCollector.rbxl`, memory-only, live-synced from `rojo serve haunted.project.json` on `task/TASK-0024-FullLoop-Landmark-Flake`. `LandmarkService.blocker` and the `LandmarkStart` scenario were confirmed present in Studio before the runs.
- Original symptom: "landmark records clue" failed with no detail in 1 of 3 FullLoop runs on the TASK-0023 build ([[TEST-20261011-08]], revision 1).

| FullLoop × 5 in a row, with the new blocker detail | — | **81/81 each, 5/5**; the landmark check passed every time | not reproduced |

## Observations
- In total the flake has shown once in 20 runs (8 FullLoops and 12 probes) across this and the previous test. The cause is still **unknown**.
- From now on, a failure reports its reason: "too far: N studs (root at …)", "no anchor", "no character root", "character was replaced" or "profile table was replaced". If none of these appear and the clue still isn't recorded, the cause lies after the guards.
- Untested hypothesis: the failing run in TEST-08 was the first play session after a Rojo resync (a cold start: script compilation and the first client load), which none of the runs here reproduced exactly.
