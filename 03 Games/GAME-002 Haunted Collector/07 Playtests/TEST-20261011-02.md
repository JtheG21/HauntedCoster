## Z-fighting
- **Main cause:** GAME-001's Baseplate (top at y = 0) was coplanar with the graveyard ground across the whole map. Removed, and `GraveyardBuilder` now deletes any stray Baseplate.
- A coplanar-top scan of ~2,100 parts then found: adjacent plots' shared boundary walls (exactly coincident), duplicate fence corner posts, mausoleum columns level with the body, pen-bar corners, track ties on tight bends, and road/path strips at equal heights where they cross. All fixed. The re-scan showed **no coplanar overlaps** among 1,976 parts.
