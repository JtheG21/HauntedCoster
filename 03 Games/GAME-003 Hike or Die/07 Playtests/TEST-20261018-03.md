## Camera and movement input fix (2026-10-18)
jg reported that movement controls felt unstable while the selfie camera was raised and the player was trying to frame the cryptid. Inspection found that the global touch-movement handler used one shared last-touch position, so a second finger moving the left thumbstick could be interpreted as camera drag. The client now starts camera drag only from an unprocessed touch in a right-side aim zone and applies movement only from that same `InputObject`; touches from the left movement stick cannot replace or move the active camera finger. The raised-camera hint now explicitly says to drag on the right and move with the left stick.

In Studio Play with the camera open, holding forward while adjusting aim with the right arrow moved the character from `(0.24, 11.01, 52.93)` to `(-0.84, 11.01, 52.94)` in 0.9 seconds; the camera stayed Scriptable and the raised-camera controls remained active. This verifies desktop movement concurrent with camera aim, not mobile multitouch. The iPhone 17 Pro landscape simulator showed the controls and revised hint on screen, but this tool has no touch injection; a physical two-thumb test remains needed. Studio was stopped and the simulator reset to default.

After this fix, `stylua --check games/hike-or-die/src`, Selene, the HOD Rojo build and `git diff --check` passed. Full mobile movement and framing feel remain for manual device verification.

## Preserve movement while framing (2026-10-18)
Reproduced jg's keyboard report in the matched desktop Studio run. Before the change, with the selfie camera raised, holding W for 0.9 seconds moved the hiker from `(0, 11.01, 52)` to `(-1.96, 11.01, 53.92)` and turned the hiker from facing `-Z` to approximately `(-0.98, 0, -0.20)`, which also swung the camera. The camera faces back toward the hiker, so Roblox's default camera-relative movement made W act like backward movement.

The client now temporarily disables `Humanoid.AutoRotate` while the camera is raised and converts the default camera-relative movement vector back into the hiker's local forward/right axes. Closing the camera restores the previous AutoRotate setting. After the change, W for 0.9 seconds moved from `z=52` to `z=38.13` (forward along the hiker's `-Z` facing); the facing remained `-Z` and the camera remained steady. Holding W while pressing the right-aim key moved forward another 13.87 studs while aim yaw changed by 3 degrees; the hiker facing stayed `-Z`. Closing the camera restored `CameraType=Custom` and `AutoRotate=true`.

This verifies keyboard movement and aiming together in Studio. Touch movement uses the same remapping code, but two-thumb movement on a physical device remains unverified. The current change passed targeted HOD checks and the full repository check; see the task note for the exact run results.
