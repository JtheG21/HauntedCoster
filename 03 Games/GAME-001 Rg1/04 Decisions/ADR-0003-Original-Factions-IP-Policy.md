# ADR-0003: Original factions inspired by anime archetypes (no franchise IP)

## Context
The four named franchises are owned IP. On Roblox, anime games have been taken down under the DMCA, and a taken-down game generally isn't restored without the owner's permission. Licensed partners exist (an official One Piece game is on Roblox). Evidence: [[RSH-20261010-Anime-IP-And-Demand]].

## Options considered
1. Use the real names, characters and moves: the highest short-term appeal, the highest takedown risk (it can wipe out all the work and revenue).
2. "Letter-swapped" names that keep the likenesses: common, still recognisably copied, and a medium to high risk.
3. **Original factions built on genre archetypes** (ninja, energy warrior, pirate, spirit swordsman), with our own names, designs, ability names, VFX colours and sounds. Players draw the comparison themselves.

## Decision (proposed; needs approval)
Option 3. Specifically:
- No franchise **names, character names or likenesses, logos, signature technique names, music or quotes**, in game, title, thumbnail, description or tags.
- Archetype-level inspiration is fine (ninjas cloning themselves, energy blasts, stretchy grabs, spirit swords are genre-wide tropes). Exact distinctive combinations are not (a specific costume, hairstyle and move set).
- Marketing hook: "**Which power is strongest?**" rather than franchise names.

## Consequences
Less instant name recognition. In exchange, the game can't be taken down and the money it earns is ours to keep. Our own faction names and art become brand assets.
