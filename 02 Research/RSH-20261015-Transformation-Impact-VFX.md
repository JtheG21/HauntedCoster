> Draft. Individual verdict: useful for buildup, peak and aftermath contrast; unsuitable as a literal everyday capture-effect target.

## Evidence limits
Local Watch; WhisperX small detected no speech. All 10 scene-selected frames and eight 3-second cue frames were directly viewed as contact sheets. The second pass resolved the purple aftermath missing from the first selection. Evidence spans the opening through the fade at 0:21. Short flashes are sampled, not measured frame by frame; no authoring or engine interface is shown. Confidence is medium for the visible sequence, low for technique and timing precision.

## Observed mechanics (verified)
- **0:00 (shown on screen):** opening is dark, with a watermark.
- **0:03–0:06 (shown on screen):** a humanoid floats inside a bright yellow central shape, surrounded by orbit-like lines, small spheres and outward-pointing accents.
- **0:08 (shown on screen):** a close-up places the character's upper body against the yellow light.
- **Around 0:09 (shown on screen):** selected images switch between white-background black silhouette and inverted, high-contrast forms.
- **0:11–0:12 (shown on screen):** broad white light, horizontal pale bands and radial elements obscure much of the character.
- **Around 0:14–0:15 (shown on screen):** radial shapes and orbit motifs resolve into a purple circular aura behind the humanoid.
- **0:18 (shown on screen):** wider framing shows purple concentric rings, angular luminous lines and suspended debris-like objects.
- **0:21 (shown on screen):** the image is substantially darker while the character and faint circular forms remain.

## Hypotheses
- A clear buildup → single emphasis peak → quieter hold could communicate a rare reveal without constant visual noise. **Confidence: low.** Test a restrained storyboard and compare player recognition with a version that has no full-screen flashes.

## Relevance to our game
Potential pacing input for [[SYS-Haunted-Capture-Loop]] or an original cosmetic reveal; no new transformation mechanic is proposed. Checked [[VFX References]] and linked existing [[RSH-20261014-Impact-Frames-Video]] and [[RSH-20261014-Animation-Event-Ability-System-Video]]. No new technical recipe is verified here.

**Transferability:** contrast between anticipation, peak and recovery can transfer. Anime-scale spectacle, long interruption and near-total screen coverage would need substantial adaptation for a multiplayer collector game.

## Original implementation ideas
An optional rare-exhibit reveal: brief local anticipation around the exhibit, one localized accent, then a low-intensity display state. Keep the subject readable throughout.

## Recommendations (max 3)
1. **Adapt** escalation and recovery as a storyboard principle.
2. **Exclude** repeated screen-filling black/white inversion and direct character/effect copying from the proposed game brief; it obscures play and needs a separate accessibility review if ever pursued.
3. Verify timing, cleanup, visibility for observers and measured cost in a future scoped effect task.

## Visual check needed
0:08–0:15 requires denser frames to measure flash count, durations and exact transition order. No flash frequency or safe-exposure claim is made.
