# Candidate B: co-op anomaly-detection horror

## 1. Concept summary
Players work a "shift" in a familiar place and must notice what's wrong (changed objects, impostor visitors) and react correctly before the anomalies escalate. Mild scares, not gore.

## 2. Target audience hypothesis
Ages about 10–16 who like light horror and playing with friends, plus streamer and YouTube audiences. *(medium confidence)*

## 4. Core gameplay loop
Start a shift → inspect visitors or rooms → accept or reject, or report the anomaly → mistakes raise the danger → survive the shift → unlock the next shift, a class or an upgrade.

## 5. What the references do well
Memory and observation skill that works for every age, tension without combat, very good for clips, and new anomaly types give each update a hook.

## 6. Weaknesses / underserved needs / differentiation
- One dominant game. Copying its setting is risky in both IP and reputation terms.
- Content treadmill: every anomaly is hand-made content.
- **Differentiation:** a different familiar setting (for example a night-shift arcade or a lighthouse), *role asymmetry* (one player watches cameras while another acts), and procedural combinations of anomalies.

## 7. Retention & social mechanics
Shift progression, classes with abilities, co-op roles, anomaly "journal" collection, weekly new anomalies.

## 8. Monetization hypotheses
Cosmetic uniforms, flashlight skins, a revive developer product (a fairness concern in co-op; to test), private servers.

## 9. Minimum viable prototype
One room, 10 anomaly variants built from simple models, 3 shifts, accept/reject input, a 2–4 player co-op shift.

## 10. Development risks & effort
- **Art and atmosphere are the product.** Lighting and audio quality strongly affect the fun, and that's our weakest area (no art budget).
- Making each anomaly takes time. Scaring young players needs age-appropriate tuning.
- Prototype effort: about 2–3 weeks part-time (rough estimate).

## 11. Evidence gaps & experiments
- Can primitive or low-poly art still create tension? Do a blind playtest with 5 people.
- How much of the demand belongs to one breakout game versus the subgenre? Track the top 10 anomaly games' CCU for 4 weeks.

## Recommendations
1. A strong #2 option. Test whether atmosphere is possible with free or simple assets before committing.

## Needs human review
- The age suitability of horror for the intended audience.
