# Candidate C: round-based party / disaster minigames

## 1. Concept summary
Short rounds (1–3 min) of rotating challenges or disasters in a shared server. The last players standing get points, then the next round loads.

## 2. Target audience hypothesis
A broad casual audience, ages about 8–14, who play in public servers with or without friends. *(medium)*

## 3. Comparable experiences (accessed 2026-10-10)
| Game | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Natural Disaster Survival (2008) | 4.5B visits, 91% rating, CCU readings from about 8K to 38K, peaking at 69K (Sep 2026) | https://www.roblox.com/games/189707 · https://bloxism.com/natural-disaster-survival/analytics · https://spawnscope.com/games/natural-disaster-survival/ |
| Squid Game X | 2.2B visits, about 17.9K CCU | https://www.robloxtracker.net/tags/party-casual/minigame/visits |
| Epic Minigames | 2.35B visits, about 1.9K CCU now | same |
| Most other minigame-tagged games | 0.3K–8.8K CCU | same |

## 4. Core gameplay loop
Lobby → round starts → survive or compete → win points or currency → spend on cosmetics or emotes → next round (no downtime).

## 5. What the references do well
Zero learning curve, natural social chaos, sessions that can last a long time, and new minigames are cheap to add one at a time.

## 6. Weaknesses / underserved needs / differentiation
- **Evergreen but crowded.** Most new entries stay small, and incumbents have decades of familiarity.
- Hard to tell apart in a thumbnail. It needs a strong hook (theme or mechanic).
- Retention depends on a large and growing minigame catalogue.

## 7. Retention & social mechanics
Daily challenge rotations, cosmetic progression, party queue with friends, seasonal events.

## 8. Monetization hypotheses
Cosmetics, emotes, a VIP pass with cosmetic perks, private servers. Avoid paid advantages in competitive rounds.

## 9. Minimum viable prototype
Round manager, a lobby, and 3 minigames (each one module), plus a points leaderboard.

## 10. Development risks & effort
Low technical risk per minigame. The main risk is **discoverability without a hook**. Prototype about 1–2 weeks part-time.

## 11. Evidence gaps & experiments
Which hook would differentiate it? Test 2–3 themes with thumbnail and title variants (needs a live listing; later milestone).

## Recommendations
1. Lower risk technically but **weak differentiation**. A good fallback, or a module inside another concept.
