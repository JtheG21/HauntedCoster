## Observed mechanics (verified)
- The original Gate 1 question is whether approaching a threatening cryptid for higher-value evidence creates understandable, fair, replayable tension. Gate 1 calls for one readable handmade trail, one observe/provoke/pursue/disengage cryptid, a tested camera and input path, server-validated explainable evidence, extraction/restart, at least one controlled variation, and enough instrumentation for a small observed study.
- The original brief says revise the smallest causal problem if aiming, monster cues, camera agency, or feedback fail. Pause/stop if repeated tests show players ignore photography, cannot perceive value differences after UX revisions, or engagement depends only on expensive scripted scares. No automatic advancement; Gate 2 needs explicit review.
- The brief explicitly excludes production lobby/co-op, persistence, monetization, public release, multiple polished cryptids/biomes, complex gallery/journal, extensive asset import, and unrelated game work from Gate 1.
- Later jg-approved scope narrowed TASK-0087 to an existence-stage merge while explicitly deferring broken camera repair, physical touch and successful full-loop proof. TASK-0087 is done and the prototype merged; this is not evidence that original Gate 1 succeeded or that launch is approved.
- Verified partial implementation: three cryptid spawn positions and rotating roam start; local console event counts. These do not amount to cohort analytics, event delivery, or a proven repeatability study. [[ADR-0014-Hike-Or-Die-Gate-1-World]] and [[ADR-0015-Hike-Or-Die-Evidence-Trust-Boundary]] were approved by jg 2026-10-18; ADR-0014 approval is limited to the Gate 1 experiment.
