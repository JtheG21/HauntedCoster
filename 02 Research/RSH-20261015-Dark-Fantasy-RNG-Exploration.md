## Observed mechanics (verified)
- **0:00–0:01 (shown on screen):** a third-person avatar exits a dark barred/stone threshold toward a warmly lit medieval street.
- **0:03–0:06 (shown on screen):** a cool blue ruin uses bright cyan lamps, arches and a visible stone path to establish a contrasting area.
- **0:07–0:09 (shown on screen):** a horned skeletal enemy appears beneath a boss health bar; a red ground strip and a later damage number are visible. This is not proof of the combat rules.
- **0:09 (shown on screen):** warm village roofs, hanging lamps and a central dark structure provide landmarks behind the avatar.
- **0:12 (shown on screen):** a chest presentation and giant mushrooms appear in separate views; surrounding vegetation and stonework use strong color contrast.
- **0:14–0:15 (shown on screen):** an avatar follows a path through a misty, mushroom-filled area.
- **0:21 (shown on screen):** a loot-bag screen requests tapping; it states that taps can upgrade the bag. Actual odds and rewards are not demonstrated.
- **0:00–end (speaker claims; one untimed transcript segment):** boss fights, rolled weapons, chest armor and quests form the advertised loop. The narrator praises the game and describes it as not yet released at recording time; no present release-status claim is made here.

## Hypotheses
- Distinct warm/cool landmark groups and visible path surfaces could help players recognize habitat transitions. **Confidence: low.** Test navigation without arrows in a greybox/lighting study.

## Relevance to our game
[[SYS-Haunted-Capture-Loop]] already has an approved collection loop; RNG combat is not a replacement. Checked [[Map References]]; its older map references remain on hold, so no comparison with those originals is claimed. Existing economy discovery: [[RSH-20261013-Haunted-Monetization-Discovery]].

**Transferability:** landmark silhouettes, lighting contrast and path framing may transfer. Weapon rolls, bosses and repeated loot tapping belong to a different gameplay proposition.

## Original implementation ideas
Explore a haunted route with a readable path, one silhouette landmark and a distinctive light treatment per habitat, using original geometry and the approved game palette.

## Recommendations (max 3)
1. **Adapt** landmark and lighting relationships rather than the exact map, textures or character identities.
2. **Exclude** rolling, loot-tapping and combat-loop changes from this research intake's scope.
3. Test visibility and route recognition in actual game lighting before treating the approach as proven.
