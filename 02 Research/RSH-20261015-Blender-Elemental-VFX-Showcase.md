## Observed mechanics (verified)
- **0:00, 0:03 (shown on screen):** a circular stone display contains several separate effect stations, each framed by a glowing floor ring.
- **0:06 (shown on screen):** a gold station uses stacked floating rings and a bright vertical region; different stations keep distinct palettes and silhouettes.
- **0:07, 0:09 (shown on screen):** a brick barrier appears intact in one selected view and broken into outward-spread pieces in another. Collision or gameplay damage is not shown.
- **Around 0:11 (shown on screen):** angular white lightning lines surround a dark upright column, with a halo and luminous markings; several selected images have different bolt contours.
- **0:12 (shown on screen):** the gold column combines vertical streaks, horizontal rings and scattered bright motes.
- **0:15–0:18 (shown on screen):** wider views expose multiple effect silhouettes and their surrounding floor space.
- **Throughout the showcase (on-screen creator claim):** the overlay attributes the VFX to Blender and a Roblox game. No authoring, export or Roblox import interface is demonstrated.

## Hypotheses
- Separating a base marker, main shape and sparse accent could make an original containment effect easier to read. **Confidence: low.** Test with one concept and an in-game capture at normal viewing distance.

## Original implementation ideas
A restrained original exhibit marker with one footprint ring, one readable ghost silhouette and a brief accent on capture completion. This is a proposal; no asset or code is copied.

## Recommendations (max 3)
1. **Adapt** layered shape hierarchy and bounded floor footprints.
2. **Exclude** exact symbols, arena composition and persistent maximum brightness from a production brief.
3. Request source/export evidence before adopting any claimed Blender technique; measure cost under the intended encounter load.
