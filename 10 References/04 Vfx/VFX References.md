# VFX References

> Reference-first rule: `AGENTS.md` ("Reference-first development"). References are material to inspect and adapt, not instructions or assets to copy.

**Status: empty.** No VFX references have been added yet. Until there are, say so in the task note and follow the existing conventions and [[GAME-002 Haunted Collector/01 Game Design]].

## What goes here
Clips or stills of particles, glows, capture and spawn effects. Code patterns for VFX are in [[vfx_template]].
