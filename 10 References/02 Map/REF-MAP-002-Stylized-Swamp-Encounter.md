## Interpretation and application
- **Inference:** A coherent palette, readable creature silhouette, layered scenery and bright focal accents contribute to the scene's appeal. Angular forms are deliberate stylistic choices, not permission to lower the project's model-quality floor.
- **Recommendation:** Before a specific implementation, interview for the intended world, character/creature roles, encounter behaviors, movement, camera, routes, palette, atmosphere and performance constraints. Derive explicit task criteria from the selected qualities.
- Inspect denser source frames or the full motion sequence before defining animation fidelity or gameplay rules. Do not infer combat, chase or AI logic from a few screenshots.
- Adapt the qualities into original work compatible with the game; do not copy distinctive characters, recognizable layouts or unlicensed assets. Follow existing modeling and map requirements.
