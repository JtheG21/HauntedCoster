- Use this primarily for how a world looks and feels; gameplay mechanics are not the intended takeaway.
- No exclusions or changes selected yet. Do not interpret this as approving every UI element, avatar design or mechanic.

## Interpretation and application
- **Inference:** Material richness, layered vegetation, architectural focal points, colored light and atmospheric depth contribute to the desired world presentation.
- **Recommendation:** Interview for a specific game's palette, texture treatment, silhouettes, density, lighting, fog, landmarks, traversal and player viewpoint before implementation. Clarify what the intended world should feel like, rather than assuming dark fantasy fits every game.
- Adapt selected qualities into original environments; do not copy recognizable layouts, characters or assets. Reference appearance does not establish rights to reuse its textures.
- Derive task-specific visual criteria and compare equivalent player views. Assess coherence and readability alongside visual richness; exact texture tiling, fog and light settings must be established by testing rather than guessed from screenshots.
