# Night festival plaza mood

## Identity
- **Where it lives:** chat attachment only, not on disk. The images are not in the repo and are not embedded here.
- **Source / licence:** Source: screenshots from a Nintendo title. Mood reference only; do not reproduce characters, buildings or motifs.
- **Purpose:** the mood target for the MathShow hub plaza map, used by [[SYS-Plaza-Map-Brief]].

## Observations (mood only)
- Starry violet and cyan galaxy sky over a permanent festival night.
- Cool world colours lit by warm light sources.
- Bunting strung across the space.
- A circular plaza with concentric rings.
- A lake beside the plaza with reflections.
- Terraced cliffs as the boundary.
- Bloom and haze.
- Flower-lined paths.
- Shooting stars.

## Interpretation
- **Inferences:** the warm-against-cool split is what makes the night read as festive rather than gloomy.
- **Uncertainty:** mood read from screenshots only; no scale, layout or lighting values are taken from them.

## Intent
| Element | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sky palette, warm/cool lighting, bloom/haze | Adapt | Core of the mood |
| Bunting, flower-lined paths, shooting stars | Adapt | Cheap festive detail; shooting stars stay cosmetic and pcall-isolated |
| Ringed circular plaza, lake, terraced cliffs | Inspiration only | Layout is set by the brief, not copied |
| Characters, buildings, motifs, pickups | Exclude | IP rule: no Nintendo characters, mushroom-cap houses, castle copies or star-pickup motifs |
