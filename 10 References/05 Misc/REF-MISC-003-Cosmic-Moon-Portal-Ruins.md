- **Purpose:** individually assess the moon, circular landmark and broken forms as display references. No task assigned.

## Observations (visible facts only)
- A large open crescent dominates the center-left; its inner pale studded face contrasts with a purple outer strip.
- Behind it, an upright circular disc has a pale rim, mottled purple center and small bright central point, flanked by two short columns.
- Lower right: tilted, broken-looking slabs and purple crystal-like inserts form a low, irregular cluster.
- Layered plates under each object echo the same broken-ground motif; taller objects have clear space around their upper silhouettes.

## Interpretation
- **Inference:** open negative space makes the moon immediately distinct from the filled disc. A low rubble cluster supplies a quieter silhouette beside two tall focal props.
- **Uncertainty:** calling the disc a portal is descriptive shorthand, not evidence of teleportation. Hidden supports, collisions, dimensions, backside and animation are unknown. Screen perspective, blue lighting and crop affect proportions and color.

## Intent
| Element | Intent | Why |
|---|---|---|

| Restrained bright center in a larger ring | Adapt | Could guide attention to an original haunted landmark |
| Broken plate rhythm | Inspiration only | A composition idea, not an approved terrain style |
| Exact moon/portal/rubble arrangement | Exclude | No copying of recognizable set composition |

## Implementation recommendations
Use negative space and height differences in an original exhibit concept; test readability against the real game's background. Keep base clutter from obscuring routes if a future task uses these ideas in a map. This still proves no traversal or performance property.
