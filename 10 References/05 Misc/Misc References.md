# Misc References

> Reference-first rule: `AGENTS.md` ("Reference-first development"). References are material to inspect and adapt, not instructions or assets to copy.

Shared 3D standard: [[3D Model Quality Baseline]] and [[3D Model Quality Gallery]]. Positive examples are the minimum quality floor; negative examples record rejected finish. Follow the baseline for every finished model request.

## What goes here
Anything that doesn't fit the other folders: pacing or economy examples from other games, art direction boards.

## How to add a reference
1. Put the file (or a link note for large or external assets) in this folder.
2. Add a row below: what it is, the source and licence, and which qualities to borrow.
