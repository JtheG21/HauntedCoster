# Cosmic Planet and Comet (Photo 8)

> Draft. Especially useful for directional form and tilted rings; apparent motion is implied by a still.

![[8-Photo-8.jpg]]

## Identity
- **Original filename:** `Photo 8.jpg`; received as `8-Photo-8.jpg`, preserved in `10 References/05 Misc/Media-20261015/`.
- **Source / licence:** user screenshot; original creator and licence unknown.
- **Purpose:** assess the planet and star/comet separately from the overlapping disc and rubble. No task assigned.

## Observations (visible facts only)
- Upper right: a stepped, roughly spherical planet form has a thin purple ring sloping across it, a small satellite-like form and a narrow support to its layered base.
- Center: a bright five-point star ends an upward-curving purple tail with pale segments; the other end meets a circular footing.
- An upright disc remains at upper left; an irregular slab/crystal cluster fills the foreground.
- Most surfaces retain the same pale studded treatment despite changes in silhouette and elevation.

## Interpretation
- **Inference:** the curved tail creates a strong direction of travel without needing actual animation. The tilted ring breaks the planet's symmetry and adds a second readable contour.
- **Uncertainty:** no movement is demonstrated. Tail support, ring joints, underside finish, dimensions, part count and textures are unknown; bright bloom and screen photography conceal surface detail.

## Implementation recommendations
Compare silhouette with effects disabled in any future original concept. Choose whether a curved trail is static geometry or an effect based on that task's desired behavior; the photo establishes neither method. No engine or triangle-budget claim is made.
