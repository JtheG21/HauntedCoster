# 3D Model Quality Gallery

Read [[3D Model Quality Baseline]] before production. This gallery preserves 17 rejected and 70 minimum-standard screenshots copied from jg's Desktop on 2026-10-13. Contact sheets aid browsing; open the linked original when using a reference. Screenshot order is filename order, not a quality ranking. The minimum examples are a floor, not a ceiling, and their different styles are not one combined art direction.

## Archive integrity
`3D Model Quality/manifest.json` records each original Desktop path, archived relative path, dimensions and SHA-256. All 87 copies matched their originals when checked on 2026-10-13. Originals on Desktop were left unchanged. Underlying asset authors/licences and technical mesh properties are unverified.
