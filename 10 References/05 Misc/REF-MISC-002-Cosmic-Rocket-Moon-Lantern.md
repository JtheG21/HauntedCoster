# Cosmic Rocket Moon and Lantern (Photo 6)

> Draft. Strong reference for a coordinated prop family; not evidence of game-ready construction or a new approved quality floor.

![[6-Photo-6.jpg]]

## Identity
- **Original filename:** `Photo 6.jpg`; received as `6-Photo-6.jpg`, preserved in `10 References/05 Misc/Media-20261015/`.
- **Source / licence:** user-supplied phone screenshot of a display; original creator, pack and licence unknown.
- **Purpose:** assess silhouette variety and shared visual treatment for original display props. No task assigned.

## Observations (visible facts only)
- A rocket with conical nose, bright circular window and purple fins stands behind a crescent moon; a squat domed structure is at left.
- Foreground: a tall central post and crossbar support two purple lantern-like boxes.
- Pale studded surfaces repeat across the props. Saturated purple accents mark fins, seams, openings and the crescent's outer edge.
- Each prop sits on layered, uneven pale plates; white four-point sparkles occupy the gaps.

## Interpretation
- **Inference:** common bases, surface language and accent placement unite unrelated silhouettes into one set. The hanging boxes demonstrate a useful support → crossbar → suspended object hierarchy.
- **Uncertainty:** scale unknown; no rear views, neutral lighting, wireframe or runtime measurements. Blue display lighting shifts the palette; sparkles may be added graphics or engine effects. Studs do not prove that the assets are individual Roblox parts.

## Intent
| Element | Intent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Distinct silhouettes within a consistent family | Keep closely as a quality | Variety remains readable without changing every material |
| Layered bases and localized accent | Adapt | Could unify original haunted exhibit decorations |
| Two suspended lantern forms | Inspiration only | Useful construction relationship, not a design to duplicate |
| Space identities, exact geometry and ubiquitous sparkles | Exclude from direct transfer | Haunted Collector needs its own approved direction |

## Implementation recommendations
Explore common base and accent rules in a future concept sheet. Check effects-off silhouettes and actual support geometry before considering any model finished. [[3D Model Quality Baseline]] and [[LES-0008-Model-Fidelity-And-Performance]] remain authoritative; this image establishes no budget or technical pass.
