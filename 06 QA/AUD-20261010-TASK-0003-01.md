## Findings
| # | Severity | Location | Evidence | Required correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **major** | `src/server/Services/FactionService.luau` | Balancing only happens in `assign` on `PlayerAdded`. With 2 Shinobi and 2 Ki, if both Shinobi leave, the server sits at 0/2 until new players join. That breaks the AC wording "keeps ... within 1", and nobody can use Echo Clone. It will also make Convergence Gates (TASK-0005, which needs both powers) impossible to solve. The task note doesn't document or test this, and the specs only cover joins. | Pick one: (a) on `PlayerRemoving` (deferred, excluding the leaving player), when the spread is >1, move one player from the largest faction (most recent joiner, or at a safe moment such as respawn or round start), clean up their clone and abilities, and add a spec for it; or (b) get explicit human approval to narrow the AC to "at assignment time" and record it in the task note. The auditor can't approve that change. |
| 2 | minor | `src/client/AbilityInput.luau` with `AbilityConfig.EchoClone` | Every player, including Ki, gets E bound with `Sink` plus a "Clone" touch button that always fails for Ki. E is also the default key for `ProximityPrompt`, so sinking it may block future prompts. This limitation is documented. | Track it as an M3 follow-up: faction-aware binding, and choose a key that doesn't clash with the prompt default (or check that they coexist). |
| 3 | minor | `src/server/Components/PressurePlate.luau` `setDoor` | The door state is written per plate. Two plates linked to one door will fight each other (the inactive one closes it). A plate destroyed while active leaves its door open. | Track each door's open state from all of its linked plates (any or all), and close the door when a plate is removed. At minimum, document "one plate per door". |
| 4 | minor | `tests/luau/FactionRules.spec.luau` | The specs only cover pure join sequences. There's no coverage of `FactionService` (`counts` excluding the player, the existing-attribute skip, `setFaction` rejection) or of leave scenarios. | Add specs for `setFaction` validation and a leave or rebalance case alongside the fix for finding 1. |
| 5 | minor | [[TEST-20261010-05]] | The build is listed as "working tree, pre-commit", not a commit SHA. The timing AC rests on one sample. The 4-client run didn't finish. | On re-test, record the commit SHA and take 3 or more timing samples. The 3-client result is enough for "within 1"; the 4-client gap is acceptable as an environment limit. |
| 6 | nit | `PressurePlate.evaluate` | Creates a new `OverlapParams` for each plate on each tick. The `Exclude` filter returns every part in the box (floor, decor) and each one goes through `isActivator`. The cost is O(plates × parts) at 10 Hz, which is fine at M2 scale but grows with plate count. | Reuse one `OverlapParams` and use an `Include` filter (characters plus the `EchoClones` folder), or skip plates no player is near. |
| 7 | nit | `EchoClone.handler` | `CanQuery = true` is needed, but it means future raycasts (projectiles, camera) will hit clones. The clone uses the client-reported root CFrame, so a movement exploiter can place clones anywhere, but they can already stand on any plate, so this is not a new trust gap. | Use a collision group or raycast filter for clones once raycast abilities exist. |
| 8 | nit | `FactionService.setFaction` | Changing a player from Shinobi to another faction doesn't remove their live clone (it lasts at most 6 s). `setFaction` also ignores balance (fine, since it's server-only). | Call `removeClone` (or fire a faction-changed hook) when the faction changes. |
| 9 | nit | `tests/luau/multiclient/FactionBalance.luau` | Hardcodes `"Faction"`, `{ "Shinobi", "Ki" }` and its own copy of the balance check instead of using `FactionConfig` and `FactionRules.isBalanced`. | Reuse the config and rules. |
| 10 | nit | `src/shared/AbilityConfig.luau` | Edits a Dash comment (TASK-0002 audit N1). This is outside TASK-0003's scope, but it's a comment only and it's documented. `FactionRules.luau`, the extra file, follows [[Luau Standards]] (pure rules in `<Name>Rules`), so that one is acceptable. | None. Keep cross-task fixes in `chore/` branches from now on. |

**Checked, no finding:**
- **Faction spoofing.** A client's `SetAttribute` on its Player doesn't replicate to the server (FilteringEnabled), and `canUse` reads the server's value, so the exploit doesn't work.
- **Activator tag.** Tags added on the client don't replicate, so only server-tagged parts count as activators.
- **Dead players.** Detection checks `Humanoid.Health > 0` and `GetPlayerFromCharacter`, so NPCs and dead players don't activate plates.
- **IP policy.** "Shinobi Clan", "Ki Vanguard", "Tide Corsairs", "Spirit Blades" and "Echo Clone" are generic archetype terms. There are no franchise names or signature technique names, and the colours are plain hues. This complies with [[ADR-0003-Original-Factions-IP-Policy]].
- **Strict mode and annotations.** All new modules use `--!strict` and annotate their public functions.
