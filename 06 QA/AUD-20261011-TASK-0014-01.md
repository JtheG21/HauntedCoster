# Audit: TASK-0014 attempt 1

## Scope
I reviewed the TASK-0014 parts of the GAME-002 integration (`git diff f51e10e..main`, at `a146f30`). I read these in full: `server/Services/CaptureService.luau` (carry, timer, escape, secure, leave), `server/Services/ContainmentService.luau`, `server/Services/MovementService.luau` and `shared/Rules/EconomyRules.luau`. I also read the carry-timer and home-arrow code in `client/UI/Hud.luau` (`:138-170, 249-286`), `shared/Config/Economy.luau`, `PlayerDataService.update`, the EconomyRules spec, and FullLoop steps 146-168 and 260-273.

Not covered: no Studio session, and no multi-client run exists. The play results are the executor's ([[TEST-20261011-02]]).

## Requirements
[[TASK-0014-Return-Trip-And-Containment]], AC1 to AC7. The failure rule comes from [[ADR-0005-Haunted-Collector-Direction]]: the carried capture escapes, journal and coins are kept, owned monsters are never touched.

## Evidence
- **Automated checks (re-run by the auditor):** `bash scripts/check.sh` gave **ALL CHECKS PASSED** (details in [[AUD-20261011-TASK-0012-01]]).
- **Studio (executor claims, 1 client):** FullLoop covers 0.7x carry speed, the wrong-pen rejection, securing at the own pen, the onboarding reward, speed restored after securing, no income while acclimating, income after acclimation (back-dated), and death while carrying = escape with owned monsters kept and an escape counted.
- **Race and ordering checks (auditor):**
  - Double secure can't happen. `secure` runs with no yield between the `carries[player]` check, `clearCarry` and the synchronous `PlayerDataService.update` (`CaptureService.luau:309-357`, `PlayerDataService.luau:321-329`), so a second prompt trigger finds no carry.
  - Escape is idempotent. `Died`, `CharacterRemoving`, the deadline loop and `PlayerRemoving` all go through `clearCarry`, which clears the carry and disconnects first (`:89-123, 425-453`), so the monster respawns exactly once.
  - Escape only changes `stats.escapes` (`:112-114`), so owned monsters and coins are untouched, as ADR-0005 requires.

| 3 | minor | `server/Services/EncounterService.luau:265-272` | `respawnEscaped` ignores zone `maxMonsters` and species `maxAlive`.
