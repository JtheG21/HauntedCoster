# Audit: TASK-0042 Art Pipeline and Direction (attempt 1)

## Verdict
**Pass.** No blocker or major issue. HCM1 codec parity holds (header, f32 bbox, u16 positions, octahedral normals, i16 UV/256, 0-to-1-based indices, "=" padding; lune 143/143 and `test_hcm_codec` 3/3 pass). EditableMesh/AssetService calls match creator-docs (`BatchAdd`, `CreateDataModelContentAsync`, `CreateMeshPartAsync`). Art is presentation-only: no new remotes, server colliders/lights/prompts unchanged in role. Ten minor/nit findings below; none gates the merge.

## Findings
1. **minor** `ArtBakeService.luau:27`, `init.server.luau:49`: `WaitForChild("ArtData")` has no timeout and runs synchronously before `PlayerDataService.start()`; a missing ArtData stalls all services (gameplay waiting on art). Fix: `WaitForChild("ArtData", 10)` then return, or `task.spawn` the whole body, and `pcall` the call in init.
2. **minor** `init.client.luau:12,48`, `ArtLoader.luau:11-13`: art is required at the top of client init with unbounded `WaitForChild`, and `Dressing.start()` is unguarded, so an art error or missing data blocks State/HUD. Fix: require and start Dressing inside `task.spawn(pcall(...))` after `State.start()`.
3. **minor** `MeshBuild.luau:15-49,67-77`: the EditableMesh is not destroyed when `BatchAdd`/`CreateMeshPartAsync` throws or `editable` fails midway (leaks budget during failures). Fix: wrap the build in `pcall`, `em:Destroy()` in the failure branch, rethrow.
4. **minor** `ArtBakeService.luau:73-87`: `active -= 1` is skipped if any statement after the `pcall` throws, and a hung `CreateMeshPartAsync` pins a slot forever and `Ready` never sets (client then waits 45 s per part). No underflow or lost job otherwise. Fix: put the whole job body in one `pcall` then decrement, and set `root.Ready` after a wall-clock cap (e.g. 60 s).
5. **minor** `Lod.luau:88-91`: the sweep loop is unguarded; one error kills LOD/cull for the session. Fix: `pcall(sweep)` with a warn.
6. **minor** `Dressing.luau:74-84` + `Lod.luau:28-33`: `ArtFollow` welds on LOD1 parts that are unparented and reparented; WeldConstraint offset on reactivation is unproven. Unused in 0042 (first user is CaptureService, later branch). Fix: skip LOD swapping for `ArtFollow`, or `PivotTo` before reparent; test with a moving anchor across 70 studs.
7. **minor** `Dressing.luau:60-61`: if neither the bake nor the local EditableMesh works (live game before jg picks path A/B), every lamp is invisible (collider and light remain). Documented and gated, but a regression vs the old primitive lamp. Fix: coarse primitive fallback when `ArtLoader.build` returns nil, or hold publish on the path decision.
8. **minor** `Dressing.luau:154-166`, `ArtLoader.luau:130`: one serial queue; an anchor whose asset is still baking blocks every other anchor for up to 45 s. Fix: re-enqueue anchors whose template is not Ready, or build per asset in `task.spawn`.
9. **minor** scope: `art/assets/creatures.py` (231 lines) and `ArtData/creature_hound.model.json` are in the 0042 diff although creatures are a stated non-goal (TASK-0043); `FIRST` in `ArtBakeService.luau:19` and SYS step 6 (`client/Art/Creatures`) name assets and files that do not exist in 0042. Fix: drop from 0042 or record it as pulled forward.
10. **nit** (a) `Props.luau:32,35`: collider 0.5 to 0.6 wide, `CanTouch=false`, light lowered 6.6 to 5.1 (hardcoded; duplicates ArtData `LightY/LightZ`; `lamps.py:5` docstring still says 6.6/+1.3). (b) `tests/test_hcm_codec.py:12` and `MeshCodec.spec.luau:7` keep the fixture by hand with only "==" padding covered; add a drift check and one pad-0/1 vector. (c) `MeshCodec.luau:73-75` does not check `n`/`nf` against `buffer.len` before `table.create`. (d) all HCM1 strings replicate to every client (528 KB for 4 assets, grows each task) though only the local fallback needs them.
