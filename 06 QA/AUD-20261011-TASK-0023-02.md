## Evidence
- **`scripts/check.sh`:** ALL CHECKS PASSED (re-run by the auditor at `bbc1d84`).
- **Stray files:** `games/haunted/` now holds only `src/`, `tests/` and an untracked `sourcemap.json`. That file is ignored by the root `.gitignore:8`, so nothing extra is tracked. `haunted.project.json` is the only Haunted project file.
- **Crypt avoidance (`ChaseRules.luau:43-52, 56-91`; `EncounterService.luau:372-373, 389`):** `fleeRegion` returns `(g.rect, g.crypt)` in the open graveyard and `nil` for the crypt and open ground. `fleeStep` drops any clamped candidate within 2 studs of the crypt rectangle. A sixth candidate (`math.pi`) was added, so the largest gap between candidate angles is about 71°, and from any point outside the margin at least one candidate lies in the free half-plane. Targets are never inside the margin, so a runner can't move into the crypt. The two tween ends sit outside the 2-stud margin, so a straight 7.5-stud step can at most graze a crypt corner; it can't end inside.
- **Start point inside the margin:** the start is `fleeStep(region, near, nil, 10, …, avoid)` from the player's position. If the player hugs the crypt wall, at least one of the six 10-stud candidates leaves the margin on the open side. The only spot where every candidate can be blocked is the 3-stud strip between the crypt's south wall (z −367) and the south fence (z −370), because the region clamp (z ≥ −366) pulls every candidate back into the margin. There `fleeStep` falls back to `best = at`, so the runner spawns on the player and every later step stays put. The player can grab it at once, so this fails safe (N1).
- **Spec discriminates:** `ChaseRules.spec.luau:67-78` starts 4 studs north of the crypt wall with the chaser to the north. Without `avoid`, the straight-away step (7.5 studs south) lands inside the crypt, so the spec fails if the fix is removed. The fence-region test also asserts the `avoid` result.
- **Roam tween ownership (`EncounterService.luau:113-128`):** roam keeps its own `tween` local and clears or cancels `e.tween` only while it is still that tween. A startle hop or flee step that replaced `e.tween` (`tweenTo` cancels the old roam tween first) is no longer cancelled. When a claim arrives, `claim` already cancels the roam tween, and roam then clears `e.tween`. When the roam tween ends naturally, it is cleared too. A finished startle tween is left in `e.tween`, and a later `claim` or `tweenTo` cancels it harmlessly. No new leak or stall found.
- **Grab prompt (`CaptureService.luau:202-212`):** `Remotes.allow(who, "Grab", 4, 4)` runs first (4/s, burst 4), so non-owner toasts are throttled too. Then non-owners get "That's someone else's escaped monster." and return, and the owner goes through the unchanged `grab` checks. Side effect: a player with their own chase can no longer grab it through someone else's prompt. That is fine because each runner has its own prompt.
- **New FullLoop checks:** "a second grab does nothing" (`FullLoop.luau:417`) passes through the `chases` and `carries` guards in `grab`. "grab after the deadline rejected (even when close)" (`:452`) teleports onto the runner, then calls `expireChaseForTest` and grabs before the 0.25 s tick. If the `os.clock() >= ch.deadline` check were removed, the grab would succeed and the check would fail, so it discriminates.
- **Flaky landmark check:** in FullLoop run 1, "landmark records clue" failed and then passed on rerun. Neither `LandmarkService` nor that FullLoop block is in this branch's diff, so I accept it as pre-existing flakiness (N3).
