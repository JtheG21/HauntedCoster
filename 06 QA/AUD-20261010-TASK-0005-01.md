| F1 | **major** | `ConvergenceGate.luau` `step()` (`cloneSeenAt`) | On opening, `cloneSeenAt = nil` "consumes" the Shinobi input. Only `LastKiHit` is really consumed. Example: clone at T, Ki burst at T+1 opens the gate (open until T+9), Ki bursts again at T+5 (3 s cooldown), the clone was last seen at T+6. At T+9 `canOpen` is true, so the gate reopens with a new `openingId`, and everyone in the PassZone gets another Shinobi and Ki charge. **One clone counts for two openings.** This contradicts the record's claim that one application can't reopen the gate. **Verified OK:**
- **Security:** attributes and CollectionService tags written by a client do not replicate to the server, so a client cannot fake `ClonePresent`, `LastKiHit` or `Echo_*`. `EchoClone` is the only code that adds `PlateActivator` (grep), and HeavyBlocks are not activators. KiBurst is limited to the Ki faction (`FactionService.requires("Ki")`) and needs charge ≥ `WallBreakFraction`. A player standing on the plate keeps the door behaviour but sets `ClonePresent = false`. The PressurePlate loop now stops early only once a clone is found, which is correct.
- **EchoService:** server-owned. `Echo_<power>` attributes are set only by the server. Charges are cleared when a player leaves. `reset` exists but is not wired until TASK-0006, which is expected.
- **Cleanup:** state is dropped when the tag is removed, and awards are dropped when a player leaves.
- **Polling cost:** at most one `GetPartsInPart` per gate per 0.1 s tick for the pass zone (while open) and one for the approach zone (while closed and not armed). Setting an attribute or property to an unchanged value is cheap. Fine at M2 gate counts.
