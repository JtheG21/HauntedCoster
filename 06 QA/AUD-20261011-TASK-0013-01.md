## Evidence
- **Automated checks (re-run by the auditor):** `bash scripts/check.sh` gave **ALL CHECKS PASSED** (details in [[AUD-20261011-TASK-0012-01]]).
- **Studio (executor claims):** TEST-01 recorded 10 monsters, each inside its zone's rect, and roaming working; the Bell Wraith cap was "not re-measured". FullLoop (TEST-02) covers: start rejected from far away, start rejected for a non-monster (`Workspace.Plots`), start rejected while carrying, and a well-timed release. The scenario calls `CaptureService.requestRelease` directly (`FullLoop.luau:96-120`), so the real remote, rate limit and latency path were never exercised. It doesn't run an early, late, spammed or forged release probe.
- **Remote validation:** `CaptureStart(model)` is safe. `EncounterService.get` accepts only a `Model` key that is in the server's own `encounters` table (`EncounterService.luau:232-237`), and the server then checks distance (18), Bell flag, crypt access, pen capacity, claim and the token bucket. `CaptureRelease` takes no arguments, and the server times it from its own `startedAt`. Claims are released on cancel, timeout and `PlayerRemoving` in both services.
- **Judge simulation (auditor):** with the shipped constants, a press at a random time is judged a hit **45%** of the time for every rarity. Mashing blindly every 0.21 s catches a Common **92%**, an Uncommon **63%** and a Rare **66%** of the time (3,000 trials each).

## Findings
| # | Severity | Location | Evidence | Required correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | major | `shared/Rules/CaptureRules.luau:7, 27-38`; `server/Services/CaptureService.luau:283-285`; `shared/Config/Monsters.luau:84-86` | The slack is ±0.09 s of needle time. At 2/period bar-units per second, that adds 0.225 (Common), 0.277 (Uncommon) or 0.327 (Rare) to the zone width. The effective window becomes about 0.46 of the bar for every rarity, so the rarity tuning is cancelled out. Blind mashing catches 92% of Commons and 66% of Rares, and Rares are easier than Uncommons. This defeats AC3 ("spammed releases rejected") and AC4. | Express the slack in bar units, or scale it down with the period (for example ≤0.03 s plus a measured jitter allowance). Consider making a miss also cost time or reset the needle. Add a spec that checks, for each rarity, the effective hit fraction (≤ zoneWidth + a small margin) and an upper bound on blind-mash success. |
| 2 | major | AC3; `tests/scenarios/FullLoop.luau:96-129` | The AC asks for an "exploit probe recorded". Nothing calls the real `CaptureRelease`/`CaptureStart` remotes from a client, so forged arguments, early and late presses, spam above the token bucket, and releases without a session were never exercised. | Run a client-side probe through the actual remotes (forged Model, a non-Model, presses at period/4 off-zone, 20 presses in 1 s, a release with no session) and record the results in a TEST note. |
| 3 | major | AC5; `games/haunted/tests/` | There's no variant-roll spec. `EncounterService.rollVariant` lives in a server service, so the specs can't reach it as a pure rule. | Move the roll into `shared/Rules` (for example `CaptureRules.rollVariant`). Add a seeded spec covering zone restriction (no Ashen outside the Crypt) and empirical rates within tolerance of the config. |
| 4 | minor | `server/Services/CaptureService.luau:283` | The latency estimate is `GetNetworkPing() * 2`. The client starts its needle when the CaptureStart reply arrives and the press needs one more uplink, so the true offset is one round trip. Roblox documents `GetNetworkPing` as round-trip, so ×2 over-corrects by a full RTT (for example 150 ms ≈ 0.19 to 0.27 of the bar). The wide slack (finding 1) currently hides this. Not testable in Studio, where ping is about 0. | Confirm `GetNetworkPing` semantics on a live server. Use a single RTT (or measure the offset per session from the CaptureStart round trip), then tighten the slack as in finding 1. |
| 5 | minor | `server/Services/CaptureService.luau:193-261, 434-438` | A session isn't ended when the player dies or respawns. The claim holds the monster frozen for others for up to the 20 s `SessionTimeout`, and the player gets "Already capturing" until then. `onCaptureStart` doesn't check that the humanoid is alive. | End the session (and release the claim) on `Humanoid.Died` and `CharacterRemoving`, the same way the carry does. Reject a start when Health ≤ 0. |
| 6 | minor | `server/Services/EncounterService.luau:207-223, 265-272` | `maxAlive = 1` counts only monsters in the world. While a Bell Wraith is being carried, `fillZones` can spawn a second one, and if the first escapes there are 2. TEST-01 noted the cap "not re-measured". | Count carried captures (from CaptureService) toward `maxAlive`, or skip the respawn when the cap is reached. |
| 7 | minor | `shared/Config/World.luau:84-111` | Scope says "cap 8". Zone maxima add up to 10 (TEST-01 counted 10), and escapes add more. The change isn't declared. | Set the caps to total 8, or record the new number in the task note and SYS note. |
| 8 | minor | `server/Services/CaptureService.luau:250-260`; `CaptureUI.luau:115-124` | The client receives `period` and `zoneCenter` and knows its start time, so a script can press at the exact moment every time. That can't be avoided with a client-drawn meter, but nothing on the server flags superhuman timing. | Accept for the prototype. Note it for the anti-exploit work (for example, flag sessions with near-zero timing error across many captures). |
| 9 | nit | `server/Services/EncounterService.luau:71-79` | Rolls are sequential (Spectral first), so a Crypt Ashen comes out at 0.9 × 0.3 = 27%, not the configured 30%. | Roll once against the cumulative chances, or document the order. |
