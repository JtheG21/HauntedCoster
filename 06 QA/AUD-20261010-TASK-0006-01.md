## Findings
| # | Severity | Location | Evidence | Required correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | minor | `MatchService.start` CharacterAdded handler | An in-match player who respawns during **Results** is sent to `CourseStart` (`if state == "Finale" then "Finale" else "CourseStart"`). They are then moved to Lobby when Results ends. | Map Results to `Finale` or `Lobby`, or use a per-state zone table. |
| 2 | minor | `TrialService` | A trial player who dies respawns at the default SpawnLocation, not the Trial pad, because `begin` only runs for the first character and MatchService skips ineligible players. The prompt and timeout still work, so the trial is not stuck. | On respawn, re-teleport to `Trial` while `not done[player]`. |
| 3 | minor | `TrialService` + `FactionService.rebalance` | Lobby or trial players stay switchable. If a rebalance changes a trial player's faction, `TrialPrompt` keeps the old power's text. `onAbility` uses the new faction, so pressing the prompted key does nothing until the 30 s timeout. | Refresh `TrialPrompt` when the faction attribute changes, or exclude trial players in the switch guard. |
| 4 | minor | `ConvergenceGate.resetAll` | Clearing `states` makes the next `getState` call `setBlocked(gate, true)` unconditionally. That skips the "never turn solid on a player" guard for one step. The bypass is also re-blocked on that step (expected) without the occupancy check. At match start, match players are on CourseStart, but lobby players could stand in a gate or bypass. `cloneConsumed` also resets, so a clone left on the plate counts as fresh input. | Accept for M2 (greybox). In TASK-0007 or later, preserve physical state or check occupancy on reset. |
| 5 | minor | `MatchService.tick` Countdown | Players who are dead at the moment the countdown ends are excluded from the snapshot and wait out the whole match. Each eligible→ineligible dip also restarts the countdown from zero (Countdown→Lobby→Countdown). This is harmless with `MinPlayers = 1`, but it becomes timer flapping once the minimum is raised for public play. | Note for the public-play tuning task: add a grace period or hysteresis, or include respawning players. |
| 6 | minor | Scope | The diff goes well beyond the 5 expected files (4 existing services, both init scripts, `MatchConfig`, the multi-client scenario, `CLAUDE.md`, an engineering doc). Each addition is small and justified (match-scoped gates, per-match resets, the switch guard, the onboarding hook) and is declared in the Implementation record. The `CLAUDE.md` change to the spec-running instructions (backed by [[LES-0005-Edit-Mode-Require-Cache]]) is a process doc, which AGENTS.md routes to `chore/` branches. | Acceptable as declared. Future process-doc edits should go on `chore/` branches. |
| 7 | nit | `AbilityService.onRequest` | The new early `return` after a handler error changes nothing else, because it was the last statement before the listener loop. Listeners run in `task.spawn`, so an error in one cannot break the request. "Success" means the handler didn't throw, not that the power had an effect: a weak Ki Burst still completes the trial. | None required. Document it if the trial ever needs a meaningful use. |
| 8 | nit | `MatchService` | `setState` return values are ignored (all edges used are valid). A persistent `tick` error would warn at 5 Hz. If `startMatch` succeeded but the following `setState` failed, the next tick would re-run `startMatch`. | Optional: assert on `setState`, and throttle repeated tick warnings. |
| 9 | nit | `EchoService` / match end | Echo charges are cleared at match **start** only, so the HUD shows leftover charges in the lobby after a match. Players who aren't in the match can also act on course gates (presence is scoped, inputs are not). | Optionally also reset in `onMatchEnd`. |
| 10 | nit | `MatchJoinLeave` | Calling `TrialService.complete` to skip the trial is acceptable setup and is documented as Scenario rule 0. The behaviour under test (join wait, leave fallback, next countdown) is driven by real joins and kicks. Leaves during Countdown, Finale and Results are not exercised by any scenario; they were reviewed in code only. | Optional: add a scenario where a player leaves during Results. |
